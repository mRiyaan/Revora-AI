# SOP-OPS-02: Housekeeping Standards, Cleanliness Defects & Service Recovery
- Policy Code: SOP-OPS-02
- Version: 1.2
- Department: Housekeeping
- Category: cleanliness
- Property Scope: ALL

## Section 1.1 — Cleanliness and Room Readiness Standards
Complaints citing stained linens, bathroom sanitation misses, dusty surfaces, pest sightings, delayed extra towels, or missed turn-down service require structured follow-up with the Executive Housekeeper. Mild feedback on 4-star stays may be held for Support review, whereas severe hygiene complaints or 1–2 star ratings escalate for Manager sign-off.

## Section 2.1 — Root-Cause Checklist
Housekeeping supervisors must verify:
1. Pre-check-in Room Inspection Checklist sign-off for the assigned room number and stay dates.
2. Housekeeping attendant assignment and supervisor audit record.
3. Preventive pest-control or deep-cleaning log if odors, mold, or pests are alleged.

## Section 3.2 — Approved Service Recovery Language
Public responses should thank the guest for flagging the lapse, note that the Executive Housekeeper has initiated a room-audit check with the floor supervisor, and avoid blaming individual staff members. Any complimentary upgrade or dining credit voucher requires Manager authorization and must not be promised publicly without approval.
