# SOP-FIN-01: Billing Disputes, Checkout Rate Variances & Refund Authorization
- Policy Code: SOP-FIN-01
- Version: 2.1
- Department: Finance
- Category: billing
- Property Scope: ALL

## Section 1.1 — Scope and Core Principle
This Standard Operating Procedure governs all guest complaints alleging billing discrepancies, OTA vs. checkout rate variances (for example, a confirmed booking rate of ₹5,000 compared against a checkout folio of ₹6,200), duplicate card charges, undisclosed resort fees, or refund demands across THE LEELA PALACE BANGALORE (The Leela Palaces, Hotels and Resorts) properties. A guest review alleging an overcharge is treated strictly as an unverified customer allegation until reconciled against internal financial records.

## Section 2.1 — Mandatory Evidence Verification Before Conclusion
Before any staff member concludes that an overcharge occurred or that a refund is owed, the Finance team must verify all of the following supporting records:
1. Original OTA or direct booking confirmation showing quoted room tariff, taxes, and inclusions (matched by Reservation ID).
2. Itemized checkout tax invoice / guest folio separating room tariff, GST/taxes, minibar, dining, and airport transfer charges.
3. Payment gateway or POS settlement batch log confirming the exact captured amount versus pre-authorization holds.
4. Front-desk night audit or rate-override log explaining any delta (such as ₹1,200 extra fee, early check-in, or room upgrade).

## Section 3.2 — Prohibition on Unverified Public Refund Promises
Public review responses MUST NEVER promise, confirm, or guarantee a refund, credit, or monetary compensation prior to Finance completing evidence reconciliation. Even if the guest quotes exact figures (such as ₹5,000 vs. ₹6,200) or demands an immediate refund, the public reply must remain factual, empathetic, invite the guest to share their booking reference with Finance if not already matched, and commit only to a verified invoice audit within 24 business hours.

## Section 4.1 — Refund Approval Authority Matrix
- Support Role: Strictly prohibited from approving billing escalations, issuing refunds, or promising compensation in public replies. Must route immediately to Finance or Manager.
- Finance Role: Authorized to audit folios, approve billing-dispute public responses after evidence review, and authorize verified overcharge reversals up to ₹10,000.
- General Manager Role: Required co-approval for disputes exceeding ₹10,000, disputed payment chargebacks, or multi-department escalations.
- Admin Role: Configuration and system governance only; barred from approving guest financial responses (separation of duties).
