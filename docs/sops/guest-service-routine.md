# SOP-CS-01: Routine Praise & Constructive Suggestion Response Guidelines
- Policy Code: SOP-CS-01
- Version: 1.0
- Department: Support
- Category: general
- Property Scope: ALL

## Section 1.1 — Routine Positive Review Eligibility
Reviews rated 4 or 5 stars (or 8.0+ on 10-point OTAs) containing positive feedback or minor non-critical suggestions—and carrying zero billing, safety, privacy, cleanliness-hazard, or misconduct indicators—are eligible for the Routine Review workflow. When automated routine publishing is enabled and all deterministic policy gates pass, the validated response may be published to the local sandbox channel automatically; otherwise it is held for Support or Manager approval.

## Section 2.3 — Tone, Personalization & Brand Voice
Routine replies for THE LEELA PALACE BANGALORE (The Leela Palaces, Hotels and Resorts) should be warm, concise (2–4 sentences), reference one concrete detail mentioned by the guest (such as breakfast, check-in speed, or pool ambiance), avoid robotic templates, and never include external URLs, personal phone numbers, or email addresses that violate OTA moderation policies (Booking.com and Tripadvisor Terra).
