# SOP-SEC-01: Guest Safety, Room Access, Privacy & Misconduct Escalation
- Policy Code: SOP-SEC-01
- Version: 1.4
- Department: Security
- Category: safety
- Property Scope: ALL

## Section 1.1 — Immediate Escalation Triggers
Any review or complaint alleging physical safety hazards, malfunctioning room locks or keycards, unauthorized staff entry (including entry while a Do Not Disturb sign is displayed), solo traveler security concerns, guest privacy breaches, missing valuables, CCTV misuse, food poisoning, or staff misconduct must bypass automated replies and escalate immediately to Security and the General Manager.

## Section 2.2 — Evidence Preservation & Investigation Protocol
Within 2 hours of escalation intake, the Security Lead and Duty Manager must preserve and inspect:
1. Electronic RFID door-lock interrogation audit log for the guest room covering the full stay window.
2. Corridor CCTV footage retention hold (minimum 30-day lock) for the relevant floor and timestamps.
3. Housekeeping / Maintenance master-key sign-out register and shift roster for the reported date.
4. Guest incident log or front-desk DND override records, if any exist.

## Section 3.4 — Public Reply & Non-Admission Guidelines
Public responses to safety, privacy, or misconduct allegations require Manager approval and must follow strict non-admission rules:
- Acknowledge the seriousness of the reported concern without admitting legal liability or confirming unverified allegations as fact.
- Never disclose room numbers, CCTV coverage details, or staff personal details publicly.
- Confirm that an internal Security & Management review has been initiated and provide a direct executive escalation channel for offline follow-up.
- Support and Finance roles are not authorized to approve public responses for safety, privacy, or misconduct cases.
